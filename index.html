Case study: appraisal review

A custom platform for the quality control of commercial appraisals, built with Partner Valuation Advisors and running on their portfolios today

Divider, 10 seconds.

Appraisal review workflows

  • A reviewer is tasked with a portfolio of appraisals - 150+ pages per document, in portfolios of 200

  • A reviewer will work a long checklist section-by-section, document-by-document

  • Documents (or portfolios) handled separately; rich data from appraisals not captured

Part 2, the problem, first half. About 50 seconds. The workflow is specific to this business, it is slow, and the data stays locked in the PDF. Both committees recognize this: valuation lives in these reports, research never gets the data out.

The problem with general-purpose AI

It’s trivial to upload an appraisal to an AI tool or direct an agent to review it. But:

  • Appraisers now have another block of text to go through, when they’d rather have an output that matches what they already work with (like a spreadsheet)

  • The results live in one person’s chat window - the reviewer next door can’t see them, and neither can the research team

  • Every document is a one-off: a 50-report portfolio means 50 uploads, 50 blocks of text, and nothing to roll up

Part 2, the problem, second half. About 50 seconds. The argument (Kyle, 10/5): chat tools are excellent; the way we use them introduces friction. Three frictions: results are sandboxed to one person; the output is another block of text to read; one document at a time, so a portfolio of 50 doesn’t scale. The platform answers each one in the process section: shared portfolio, fields and flags with click-to-page, and engineered cloud processing (parallel workers, background jobs, emailed workbook). Do not name Augur; the room will supply its own examples.
